** Calculations
*** starting speeds
Vis-viva equation
\[v = \sqrt{GM\left(\frac{2}{r} - \frac{1}{a}\right)}\]
- G = 6.674e-11, the gravitational constant (GRAVITATIONALCONSTANT)
- M = 1.989e30 kg, the Sun's mass (SOLARMASS), so GM ≈ 1.327e20
- r = the planet's current distance from the Sun
- a = the semi-major axis, half the longest width of the ellipse: \\
  a = (perihelion + aphelion) / 2
